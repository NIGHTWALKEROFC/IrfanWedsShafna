How it happened

Our Story

Not a straight line, but a good one — told the way we like to tell it.

Chapter I

The First Hello

It started in college, the way most good stories do — in a hallway, over a shared class, with neither of them expecting much of it. One conversation turned into another, and somewhere between lecture notes and canteen queues, Irfan and Shafna became friends.

Chapter II

Best of Friends

Years went by, and that friendship only got sturdier. Late-night calls about nothing in particular, showing up for each other's small wins and bad days — long before anyone used the word "relationship," they already had each other's back.

Chapter III

A Conversation at Home

Then came the conversations every close-knit family eventually has. Marriage talks began quietly at home, and somewhere in that discussion, both families realised they were already thinking of the same two names — Irfan and Shafna.

Chapter IV

Two Hearts, One Answer

The families asked, gently, what Irfan and Shafna thought. It turned out the two of them had quietly hoped for this too. No grand confessions were needed — just two honest "yes"es, and a lot of relieved smiles around the room.

Chapter V

Forever, Officially

Dates were set, sweets were shared, and the news travelled fast through both families. What began as a college friendship was now, officially, a wedding in the making — and everyone got to work.

Chapter VI

And So, The Story Continues…

On 26 and 27 September 2026, that story gets its next big chapter — Nikkah at Koyas Auditorium, and celebrations at Mythree Bhavan. We'd love nothing more than for you to be there when we turn the page.

Here's to forever

Thank you for being part of everything that led us here. We can't wait to celebrate with you.
